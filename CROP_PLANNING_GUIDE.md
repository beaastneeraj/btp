# Crop Planning Application - User Guide

## Overview
The Crop Planning Application is a comprehensive tool for Indian farmers to make informed decisions about crop selection, planning, and resource management. It uses scientific data, weather information, and market trends to provide personalized recommendations.

## Features

### 1. Crop Recommendations
Get personalized crop recommendations based on:
- **Current Location**: Weather and climate data
- **Field Size**: Customized calculations for your land
- **Soil Type**: Recommendations matching soil conditions
- **Season**: Best crops for current planting season
- **Market Trends**: Profitability analysis based on current prices

#### How to Use:
1. Open the Crop Planning screen
2. Configure your field parameters:
   - Adjust field size (0.1 to 10 hectares)
   - Select soil type (Loamy, Clay, Sandy, Black Cotton, Alluvial)
3. Click "Update Recommendations" to get personalized suggestions
4. View top profitable crops highlighted in green
5. Browse all suitable crops with suitability scores

### 2. Schedule Calculator
Calculate precise planting and harvesting schedules for any crop.

#### Features:
- Select from 14+ crops
- Choose your preferred planting date
- Get detailed growth stage timeline
- See harvest date prediction
- View optimal care activities for each stage

#### How to Use:
1. Click on "Crop Schedule Calculator" in the Planning Tools section
2. Select your crop from the dropdown
3. Choose planting date
4. Click "Calculate Schedule"
5. Review the growth stages with dates and descriptions

#### Growth Stages Included:
- Sowing/Planting
- Germination
- Vegetative Growth
- Flowering
- Fruit/Grain Development
- Maturity/Harvest

### 3. Field Layout Planner
Plan your field layout with crop rotation strategies.

#### Features:
- Define field area and shape
- Select multiple crops for rotation
- Get optimized field allocation
- View crop-specific requirements
- Learn about companion planting

#### How to Use:
1. Click on "Field Layout Planner"
2. Set field area using the slider
3. Select field shape (Rectangle, Square, Irregular, Circular)
4. Choose up to 3 crops for rotation
5. Click "Generate Field Plan"
6. Review the zone-wise crop allocation

#### Benefits of Crop Rotation:
- Improved soil health
- Natural pest control
- Better nutrient management
- Reduced disease pressure
- Increased overall yields

### 4. Resource Calculator
Calculate exact resource requirements for crop cultivation.

#### Calculates:
- **Seeds/Plants**: Number of plants needed, spacing requirements
- **Fertilizers**: NPK requirements in kg
- **Water**: Irrigation frequency and water needs
- **Labor**: Estimated labor days required

#### How to Use:
1. Click on "Resource Calculator"
2. Select your crop
3. Set field area
4. Click "Calculate Resources"
5. Review detailed resource breakdown

#### Resource Categories:
- **Seeds & Planting**: Plant density, row spacing, total plants
- **Fertilizer Requirements**: Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium amounts
- **Water & Irrigation**: Frequency, critical stages
- **Labor Requirements**: Estimated days, peak periods

### 5. Market Price Tracker
Track current market prices and profitability trends.

#### Features:
- Real-time price information
- Price range (min-max)
- Profitability indicators
- Season information
- Trend analysis (up/down)

#### How to Use:
1. Click on "Market Price Tracker"
2. Browse all crops with current prices
3. View profitability levels (Very High, High, Medium, Low)
4. Compare prices across different crops
5. Make informed selling decisions

#### Price Categories:
- **Current Price**: Latest market rate per quintal
- **Price Range**: Historical min-max prices
- **Profitability**: Expected profit margin
- **Trend**: Price movement indicator

## Understanding Recommendations

### Suitability Score
Crops are rated 0-100% based on:
- **Climate Match**: Temperature and humidity suitability
- **Season Timing**: Optimal planting window
- **Soil Compatibility**: Soil type and pH match
- **Water Availability**: Irrigation requirements
- **Market Conditions**: Current profitability

#### Score Interpretation:
- **80-100%**: Highly Suitable (Green)
- **60-79%**: Moderately Suitable (Orange)
- **Below 60%**: Less Suitable (Red)

### Profitability Analysis
Each crop shows:
- **Expected Revenue**: Based on average yield and current prices
- **Cultivation Costs**: Seeds, fertilizer, irrigation, labor
- **Net Profit**: Revenue minus costs
- **ROI**: Return on Investment percentage

### Risk Assessment
Risk levels consider:
- **Weather Risks**: Temperature, rainfall patterns
- **Market Volatility**: Price fluctuations
- **Pest/Disease**: Historical prevalence
- **Experience Level**: Crop complexity

#### Risk Levels:
- **Low**: Stable, beginner-friendly crops
- **Medium**: Requires some experience
- **High**: Expert-level, market-sensitive crops

## Best Practices

### Field Configuration
1. Measure your field area accurately
2. Get soil tested for pH and nutrients
3. Update configuration when conditions change
4. Use local soil type classifications

### Using Schedule Calculator
1. Plan ahead - calculate before planting season
2. Mark important dates in calendar
3. Prepare resources for each growth stage
4. Monitor weather for schedule adjustments

### Field Layout Planning
1. Practice crop rotation annually
2. Avoid planting same family crops consecutively
3. Consider water requirements when zoning
4. Leave space for pathways and drainage

### Resource Planning
1. Order resources well in advance
2. Account for 10-15% buffer in quantities
3. Store fertilizers properly
4. Plan labor for peak seasons

### Market Monitoring
1. Check prices regularly during harvest season
2. Consider storage options for better prices
3. Diversify crops to reduce market risk
4. Connect with local mandis and buyers

## Tips for Success

### General Guidelines
- Always cross-verify recommendations with local experts
- Start with smaller areas for new crops
- Keep records of inputs and yields
- Join farmer groups for knowledge sharing

### Seasonal Planning
- **Kharif (June-October)**: Rice, Maize, Cotton, Pulses
- **Rabi (October-March)**: Wheat, Chickpea, Mustard, Vegetables
- **Zaid (March-June)**: Vegetables, Fodder crops
- **Perennial**: Sugarcane (year-round)

### Weather Considerations
- Monitor forecasts during critical stages
- Plan irrigation based on rainfall
- Protect crops during extreme weather
- Adjust schedules if weather changes

### Soil Health
- Test soil every 2-3 years
- Add organic matter regularly
- Practice crop rotation
- Use appropriate fertilizers
- Maintain proper pH levels

### Water Management
- Use drip/sprinkler where possible
- Irrigate during cooler hours
- Mulch to reduce evaporation
- Collect rainwater when available
- Monitor soil moisture levels

## Troubleshooting

### No Recommendations Appearing
- Check internet connection
- Verify field configuration is set
- Try refreshing the page
- Contact support if issue persists

### Incorrect Prices
- Prices are indicative and may vary locally
- Check with local mandi for actual rates
- Consider transportation and storage costs
- Market conditions change rapidly

### Schedule Adjustments
- Weather delays may require rescheduling
- Consult local agricultural officer
- Monitor crop health indicators
- Adjust irrigation and fertilization accordingly

## Quick Reference

### Seasons
- **Kharif**: Monsoon crops (June-October)
- **Rabi**: Winter crops (October-March)
- **Zaid**: Summer crops (March-June)

### Soil Types
- **Alluvial**: River deposits, fertile
- **Black Cotton**: Deep, moisture-retentive
- **Red**: Weathered, needs fertilizers
- **Laterite**: Iron-rich, acidic
- **Sandy**: Light, well-drained

### Water Requirements
- **Very High**: >1000mm (Sugarcane, Rice)
- **High**: 700-1000mm (Banana, Rice)
- **Medium**: 400-700mm (Wheat, Maize)
- **Low**: <400mm (Pulses, Millets)
